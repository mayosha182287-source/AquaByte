01 / THE CHALLENGE

The Cooling Challenge

As AI and high-performance computing expand, data centers face increasing thermal-management demands. Cooling systems must balance reliable heat removal with responsible resource use.

Water Consumption

Some data center cooling approaches require substantial water resources. Reducing water loss and improving reuse are important sustainability challenges.

Energy Demand

Pumps, chillers, and other cooling equipment consume electricity. Unnecessary cooling demand can increase the overall energy burden.

Uneven Heat Distribution

Different computing components can produce different heat loads. A uniform flow strategy may not always match local cooling needs.

02 / OUR INNOVATION

Introducing AquaByte

Our proposed system combines intelligent monitoring, controlled water circulation, and a biochar-based stage to investigate improved thermal management and water reuse.

Intelligent Monitoring

Temperature sensors monitor the cooling loop and help identify when specific areas require additional cooling.

Adaptive Flow Management

A controlled pump and valves regulate water distribution according to monitored thermal conditions.

Rice Husk Biochar

A porous biochar-based material derived from rice husk is investigated as an intermediate component in the system.

Closed-Loop Water Reuse

The design aims to circulate and reuse cooling water, minimizing unnecessary water discharge.

03 / SYSTEM OPERATION

How Does AquaByte Work?

A proposed sequence that connects temperature detection, controlled flow, material-based heat management, and water reuse.

STEP 01

Detection

Sensors monitor water temperature and detect programmed thresholds.

STEP 02

Flow Control

Valves direct the water through the selected cooling pathway.

STEP 03

Biochar Stage

The thermal performance of the porous biochar section is evaluated.

STEP 04

Heat Exchange

A heat exchanger provides the final cooling stage when required.

STEP 05

Water Return

Water returns to the circulation loop for continued use.

GPU / Heat Source → Sensors → Biochar Stage → Heat Exchanger → Water Return
